TITLE: Beat Mitra: Postman Helper
SHORT: Offline helper for postmen: find addresses, plan routes, record deliveries.
---
Beat Mitra is a free, offline helper for postmen. Learn a delivery beat fast, find any address in seconds, plan the day's route and record every delivery – all on your phone, without internet.

Independent helper tool for postmen. Not an official Department of Posts app. Follow your office's rules on customer information.

FIND ANY ADDRESS
• One search box for name, door number, street, building, landmark, area or PIN
• Kannada, Hindi and English spellings all match: "ರಮೇಶ್", "Ramesh", "Rameshh", "रमेश"
• Door numbers like 12/3, 12-3 and #12/3 are understood
• Voice search
• Big arrow and distance to the house – works without internet

LEARN THE BEAT
• "Add here" at every door: GPS, 1–3 photos, door number, names, landmark and notes such as "dog" or "gate locked"
• Streets in your usual walking order
• Flashcards and walk mode for new and relief postmen

TODAY'S ARTICLES
• Scan article barcodes (S10 numbers checked automatically)
• Photograph the address – the app reads English and Hindi print on the phone and suggests the matching house
• Registered, Speed Post and money orders marked "needs signature / OTP"

ROUTE AND DELIVERY
• Shortest route or your street order, drag to reorder
• Big "Next stop" card: Delivered / Not delivered with reason / Skip / Navigate
• Day summary with counts and re-attempts for tomorrow

MY ROUTES
• Your walk is recorded during delivery
• A new postman can follow the same path step by step, or follow it back to the post office
• Open routes and "back to start" in Google Maps

HANDOVER AND BACKUP
• Give your beat to a relief postman as a password-protected encrypted file
• Full encrypted backup and restore

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Names and addresses stay only on your phone, encrypted
• PIN / fingerprint lock, optional screenshot blocking
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no internet permission
• Free, with no in-app purchases

Languages: ಕನ್ನಡ, English, हिन्दी
