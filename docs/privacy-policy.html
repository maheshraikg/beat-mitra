Beat Mitra (ಬೀಟ್ ಮಿತ್ರ · बीट मित्र) – Privacy Policy

Englishಕನ್ನಡहिन्दी

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · App ID: com.beatmitra.app

Beat Mitra is an independent helper tool for postmen. It is not an official Department of Posts / India Post app. Follow your office's rules on customer information.

Summary

Everything you enter in Beat Mitra stays on your phone, encrypted. The app has no server, no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The Play Store version of the app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

Information stored on your phone

  • Beat information you type or record: streets, door numbers, buildings, landmarks, PIN codes, notes, names of people who receive mail at a place and, optionally, their phone numbers.
  • Photos of houses that you take in the app (compressed, encrypted, never added to the gallery).
  • Location (GPS) of saved places, the time and place of each delivery action, and recorded walking routes.
  • Delivery records: article numbers, article type, delivery status and reason.

This data is kept in an encrypted database (SQLCipher, AES-256) with a key held in the Android Keystore, and is protected by your app PIN. It is excluded from Google cloud backup. Delivery history is deleted automatically after the number of days you choose (default 90).

Permissions and why they are used

  • Location: to save where a place is, to point the arrow to a house, to plan routes and to record your walking route. While a route is being recorded, location is used with the screen off; a notification is always shown and recording stops when you press "Stop & save". Location is never sent off the phone.
  • Camera: to take house photos, scan article barcodes and read printed addresses. Barcode and text reading run on the phone (Google ML Kit on-device models).
  • Microphone: only when you tap the microphone for voice input. Speech is converted to text by your phone's speech service. Depending on your phone's settings, that service (for example Google) may process audio online; install the offline language pack to keep it on the phone.
  • Fingerprint / face: optional, to unlock the app. The app never sees your fingerprint.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show that route recording is running.

Sharing

Beat Mitra does not share or sell any data. Data leaves your phone only when you choose to:

  • export a beat for a relief postman or make a backup – these files are encrypted with a password you choose;
  • share a day summary as text (it contains article numbers and reasons, not names);
  • share a route as a GPX file;
  • open a place or route in another app such as Google Maps or the phone dialer – that app's own privacy policy then applies.

Your control

  • Edit or delete any place, route or article at any time.
  • Settings → "Delete all data" erases everything, or uninstall the app.
  • Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing for us to delete or hand over.

Children

Beat Mitra is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.
